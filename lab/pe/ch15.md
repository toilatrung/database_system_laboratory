## 15.10
Consider the extended relational-algebra operators below. For each one, explain how it can be implemented with sorting and with hashing.

### a
Semijoin (⋉<sub>θ</sub>): in the multiset semijoin r ⋉<sub>θ</sub> s, a tuple r<sub>i</sub> occurring n times in r occurs n times in the result if at least one tuple s<sub>j</sub> exists such that r<sub>i</sub> and s<sub>j</sub> satisfy θ; otherwise r<sub>i</sub> does not occur in the result.

### b
Anti-semijoin (⊳̄<sub>θ</sub>): in the multiset anti-semijoin of r and s, a tuple r<sub>i</sub> occurring n times in r occurs n times in the result if no tuple s<sub>j</sub> of s satisfies θ together with r<sub>i</sub>; otherwise r<sub>i</sub> does not occur in the result.
