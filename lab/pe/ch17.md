## 17.8
A *lost update* anomaly happens when a transaction T<sub>j</sub> reads a data item, another transaction T<sub>k</sub> then writes that item (possibly based on an earlier read), and afterwards T<sub>j</sub> writes the item. The update made by T<sub>k</sub> is lost, because T<sub>j</sub>'s update did not take into account the value T<sub>k</sub> wrote.

### a
Give a schedule that exhibits the lost update anomaly.

### b
Give a schedule showing that the lost update anomaly can occur under the **read committed** isolation level.

### c
Explain why the lost update anomaly cannot occur under the **repeatable read** isolation level.
